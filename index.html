Visual effects and post-production, out of New York.

Mind VFX LLC is an independent studio in Elmhurst, Queens. Since 2018 we have delivered compositing, motion graphics and finishing work for advertising, branded content and independent film.

Services

Visual Effects

Compositing, clean-up, rotoscoping, set extension and CG integration for commercial and narrative work.

Motion Graphics

Title design, animated brand systems, explainer and social-first pieces built for delivery across formats.

Post-Production

Editorial finishing, color, versioning and final delivery to broadcast, web and platform specifications.

Content Production

End-to-end production support for advertising campaigns, from concept through final master.

About the company

Mind VFX LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of New York and operating since 2018. The studio is owned and directed by Rafael E. Bonilla and works with agencies, production companies and direct clients in the United States and Latin America.

Our office is located at 6032 Wetherole St., Elmhurst, New York, in Queens County. Enquiries are handled by phone and email during business hours, Monday through Friday.
